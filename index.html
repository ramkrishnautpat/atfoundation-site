Who we are

Building stronger futures, one game at a time.

AT Foundation (ATF) is a non-profit initiative founded by Anju Turambekar on 22 February 2022. Based in the Gadhinglaj region of Kolhapur district, Maharashtra, ATF works to connect rural communities with opportunities through sport, education and social empowerment.

We believe football can be a practical classroom for teamwork, decision-making, discipline and respect. Through community-based programmes, we aim to reduce barriers to participation and create pathways for children and young people—particularly girls—to learn, grow and lead.

Our direction

A clear purpose. A community-led approach.

We want every child to have the space, support and encouragement to discover what they can become.

01 / Vision

An inclusive society through sport

To create an inclusive society where every child and young person, irrespective of gender, background or economic circumstances, has the opportunity to learn, grow and lead through sports.

02 / Mission

Turn participation into possibility

To use football and other sports for holistic development—expanding access, promoting gender equality, building leadership and supporting talent while connecting sport with education, wellbeing and social responsibility.

What we do

Programmes that go beyond the pitch.

The person behind the purpose

From rural roots to building opportunities for others.

Anju Turambekar hails from Bekanal village in Gadhinglaj taluka, Kolhapur district, Maharashtra. Her journey in football reflects perseverance, continuous learning and a commitment to widening access for the next generation.

She founded AT Foundation on 22 February 2022, inspired by her own experiences and the barriers faced by aspiring athletes in rural communities. Through ATF, her ambition is to build an environment where children—especially girls—can become better players, confident individuals and future leaders.

Her vision is bigger than football results: it is about helping children gain the skills and opportunities to shape their own futures.
